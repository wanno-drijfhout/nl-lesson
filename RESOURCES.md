# Dutch Resources

Every link below was checked on 2026-09-15 unless marked (unverified). Entries from the 12 Sep 2026 session are kept.

## Knowledge

### Levels and exams
- [Reference: Europass CEFR self-assessment grid (PDF)](https://europass.europa.eu/system/files/2020-05/CEFR%20self-assessment%20grid%20EN.pdf)
  The official one-page A1–C2 grid across five skills. Use for: self-rating, and understanding what any "B1" claim means.
- [Reference: Taalunie ERK-Nederlands](https://erk-nederlands.taalunie.org/over-het-erk/)
  Taalunie's hub for the CEFR as applied to Dutch, plus the [Dutch descriptor supplement (PDF)](https://taalunie.org/feeds/download/erk-supplement-met-nieuwe-descriptoren-5dcaa.pdf/ERK%20Supplement%20met%20nieuwe%20descriptoren/original). Use for: level definitions in Dutch, once reading at B1+.
- [Official: DUO, Staatsexamen NT2](https://www.duo.nl/particulier/staatsexamen-nt2/hoe-het-staatsexamen-nt2-werkt.jsp)
  Programma I = B1, Programma II = B2. Four computer-based parts. Use for: the milestone exam on the mission. Sample exams at [staatsexamensnt2.nl](https://www.staatsexamensnt2.nl/).
- [Official: Inburgeren.nl (English)](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/)
  Civic-integration routes and exams. Use for: reference only, if a move becomes concrete. Ukrainians under temporary protection are currently exempt.

### Placement tests
- [Test: NT2.nl Woordenschattoets (free, A1–B2)](https://www.nt2.nl/en/toetsen/volwassenen/woordenschattoetsen)
  70-question vocabulary size test from publisher Boom, gives a CEFR indication. Use for: a second, independent check after lesson 0001.
- [Test: Learndutch.org level test](https://www.learndutch.org/)
  Bart de Pau's free quick test. Use for: rough gauge only.

### Grammar and usage
- [Reference: e-ANS, Algemene Nederlandse Spraakkunst](https://e-ans.ivdnt.org/)
  The authoritative reference grammar of Dutch, in Dutch. Use for: settling any "why" question. The teacher cites this for grammar claims.
- [Reference: Taaladvies.net](https://taaladvies.net/)
  Joint advice service of Taalunie, IVDNT and Onze Taal. Use for: "is this correct?" on spelling, grammar, word choice, NL/BE differences. First stop.
- [Reference: Onze Taal Taalloket](https://onzetaal.nl/taalloket)
  Advice desk of Genootschap Onze Taal. Use for: style and register questions in formal writing.
- [Reference: Dutchgrammar.com](https://www.dutchgrammar.com/en/grammar.php)
  Free English-language grammar course with audio. Blocks automated fetching (403), open in a browser. Use for: A1–B1 rule lookups with clear English explanations.
- [Reference: Zichtbaar Nederlands](https://www.zichtbaarnederlands.nl/)
  Visual grammar with English versions. Use for: when a rule from Dutchgrammar.com is not clicking.
- [Reference: Learndutch.org grammar lessons](https://www.learndutch.org/dutch-grammar/)
  50 free structured video lessons in English. Use for: A1–A2 grammar by video.

### Dictionaries
- [Dictionary: Van Dale](https://www.vandale.nl/)
  The standard Dutch dictionary. The free online tier closed on 10 December 2025 (checked 2026-09-25); paid subscription only. Use for: register labels (informeel/formeel) if a subscription is ever bought. Free fallback: NL Wiktionary below.
- [Dictionary: Woordenlijst.org (Groene Boekje)](https://woordenlijst.org/)
  Official Taalunie spelling list with word forms. Use for: spelling, plurals, verb forms in formal writing.
- [Dictionary: Lingea Oekraïens–Nederlands](https://dict.com/oekraiens-nederlands)
  Free Ukrainian–Dutch dictionary, ~218k entries. Use for: bilingual lookup from Ukrainian.
- [Dictionary: Glosbe Dutch–Ukrainian](https://glosbe.com/nl/uk)
  Corpus-based, with example sentences. Use for: collocations. Quality varies.
- [Dictionary: NL Wiktionary](https://nl.wiktionary.org/wiki/WikiWoordenboek:Hoofdpagina)
  Community dictionary with etymologies. Use for: word origins and Ukrainian translations of common words.

### Courses and textbooks
- [Course: University of Groningen, Introduction to Dutch (FutureLearn)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/dutch)
  Free 3-week absolute-beginner course. Use for: starting from zero.
- [University of Groningen — Online Dutch Courses](https://www.rug.nl/language-centre/language-courses/dutch/online-dutch?lang=en)
  Structured CEFR A1–C1 courses from a Dutch university. Use for: level-graded structured learning, all four skills.
- [Maastricht University — Dutch Language Course A1–C1](https://academy.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/course/dutch-language/)
  University courses focusing on speaking and listening. Use for: in-person or online structured progression.
- [Textbook: Nederlands voor anderstaligen, Delftse methode (Boom NT2)](https://www.nt2.nl/en/productgroep/101-388_Nederlands-voor-anderstaligen)
  The classic immersion-style beginner method, 43 lessons with audio. Paid. Use for: the standard self-study path to A2.
- [Platform: NT2 TaalMenu](https://nt2taalmenu.nl/)
  Free exercises A1–B2 including Staatsexamen-style practice. Use for: drilling between levels.
- [Platform: NedBox (KU Leuven)](https://nedbox.be/)
  Free practice with real TV clips and news texts. Belgian Dutch. Use for: authentic input with support.
- [Platform: Oefenen.nl](https://oefenen.nl/)
  Nonprofit platform used in libraries. Use for: extra basic-level practice.
- [Textbook: Wikibooks Dutch](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Dutch)
  Free open course in English, uneven. Use for: a second explanation when others fail.

### Pronunciation
- [Reference: Wikipedia Help:IPA/Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Dutch)
  IPA chart with example words and audio. Use for: decoding dictionary transcriptions.
- [Tool: Forvo Dutch](https://forvo.com/languages/nl/)
  Native recordings of single words. Use for: checking how a specific word sounds.
- Dutchgrammar.com's pronunciation chapter (above) covers ui/eu/g/r with audio.

### Graded input
- [Video: NOS Journaal in Makkelijke Taal](https://npo.nl/start/serie/nos-journaal-in-makkelijke-taal/afleveringen)
  Daily 10-minute news in simple, slow Dutch. Use for: A2–B1 daily listening.
- [Video: NOS Jeugdjournaal](https://jeugdjournaal.nl/)
  Youth news, clear natural Dutch. Use for: B1 listening and reading.
- [YouTube: Easy Dutch](https://www.youtube.com/@EasyDutch)
  Street interviews with NL/EN subtitles. Use for: informal register, real speech.
- [YouTube: Dutchies to be](https://www.youtube.com/@dutchiestobe)
  Linguist-teacher, grammar and pronunciation in English. Use for: A1–B1 explanations.
- [YouTube: Bart de Pau / Learndutch.org](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC2IYiaH8iDXjJHblQIYPAyw)
  Grammar and vocabulary in English. Use for: A1–A2 video lessons.
- [Podcast: Een Beetje Nederlands](https://www.eenbeetjenederlands.nl/)
  B1/B2 podcast on Dutch culture with free transcripts. Use for: intermediate listening with text support.
- [Podcast: Zeg het in het Nederlands](https://open.spotify.com/show/1JKtddIRlFHl5RWMENmOib)
  Slow-Dutch episodes under 20 minutes. Use for: A2–B1 listening.

### Reading ladder
- [Book: *Short Stories in Dutch for Beginners* by Olly Richards](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1529367238)
  Graded reader at A2. Use for: first reading fluency.
- [Book: *Jip en Janneke* by Annie M.G. Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jip_and_Janneke)
  Classic children's books, cultural touchstone. Use for: A1–A2 reading.
- [Book: *Het Achterhuis* (Anne Frank)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Diary_of_a_Young_Girl)
  B1–B2 authentic prose. Use for: intermediate reading.
- [Book: *Het Diner* by Herman Koch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dinner_(novel))
  B2 contemporary novel, sharp dialogue. Use for: register awareness.
- [Book: *De Aanslag* by Harry Mulisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Assault)
  C1 literary Dutch. Use for: advanced reading.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/learndutch](https://www.reddit.com/r/learndutch/)
  Largest English-language learner subreddit, well-moderated. Resource wiki mirrored at [dutch.miraheze.org](https://dutch.miraheze.org/wiki/Resources). Use for: questions, corrections, exam experiences.
- [Learn Dutch Discord](https://discord.gg/8cZWufrMzY)
  Server linked from the r/learndutch wiki. Native helpers, practice channels. Moderation quality unverified. Use for: real-time practice.
- [r/thenetherlands](https://www.reddit.com/r/thenetherlands/)
  Dutch-only subreddit. Use for: B2+ reading and writing in the wild.
- [Het Begint met Taal](https://www.hetbegintmettaal.nl/)
  National volunteer coaching network, 540 locations, taalcafés in libraries. Use for: regular spoken practice if in the Netherlands.
- [Tandem](https://tandem.net/) / [HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com/)
  Language-exchange apps. Use for: Dutch natives learning English or Ukrainian.

## Gaps
- No free four-skills online placement test with an official CEFR result. Lesson 0001 plus the NT2.nl vocabulary test is the best free combination.
- No print-quality Dutch–Ukrainian learner's dictionary. Lingea and Glosbe are the best found.
- No dedicated grammar practice book (a Dutch "Grammar in Use" equivalent) identified yet.
- C1–C2 material beyond ANS, Taaladvies and native media not yet located. Candidates: NRC, De Correspondent, Onze Taal magazine.
- Do not use nederlandsetaalvooroekrainers.nl: the domain now serves a casino page.
