Why this lesson

An idiom is the last step from fluent to native. You can live without them. But colleagues say de kogel is door de kerk in a meeting and everyone nods, and you are still looking for the church. This lesson gives you fifteen that are common in offices and at kitchen tables. Each one has a page at Onze Taal with its meaning and its story. Stories make them stick.

Ukrainian hook. Some pictures are shared: de knoop doorhakken is розрубати вузол, the same Gordian knot. de handdoek in de ring gooien is the same boxing towel. One is a false friend: розставити крапки над «і» means to make everything clear. de puntjes op de i zetten means to finish the last small details.

The idea

Learn the scene, not the words. Each idiom fits one kind of moment. De knoop doorhakken: a group has talked too long and someone decides. Learn that moment.

Do not bend them. An idiom is fixed. de knoop doorhakken, not een knoop hakken. Same glue as the collocations of lesson 0005, only stronger.

First recognise, then use. For a month, only notice them. Then use two. A wrong idiom is louder than no idiom.
