Why this lesson

English is your strongest foreign language. So English is what leaks into your Dutch: een besluit maken, fixen in a formal letter. The words are Dutch. The shape is English. Onze Taal keeps a public list of these, each with a verdict: accepted, doubtful, or better not. This lesson takes fourteen "better not" items from that list and gives you the Dutch fix for each. It also shows you which ones have become fine, so you do not over-correct.

Ukrainian hook. Your Ukrainian is often closer to Dutch than your English is. мільярд = miljard. абзац = alinea, not "paragraph". приймати рішення = een beslissing nemen. тверезий = nuchter. When English and Ukrainian disagree, try the Ukrainian shape first.

The idea

Translate the idea, not the words. "At the end of the day" is one idea: uiteindelijk. Five English words, one Dutch word.

The verdict moves. Onze Taal says the label is always a bit subjective, and that it can fade: baby, computer and penalty are simply Dutch now. Onze Taal, anglicismen

Do not panic. Taaladvies avoids the label altogether and reminds us that many loans are fully accepted. zich realiseren got its "become aware" meaning from English and is normal Dutch today. Taaladvies
