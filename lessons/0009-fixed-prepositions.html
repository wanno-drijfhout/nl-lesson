Why this lesson

Lesson 0005 glued nouns to verbs. This lesson glues verbs to prepositions. You wait op someone, think aan someone, love van someone. The preposition is not logical. It is part of the word. At B2 these are the errors that stay longest, because your sentence is understood anyway and nobody corrects you. Ukrainian has the same glue, and sometimes it matches Dutch. The table marks where it helps.

The idea

A fixed preposition means almost nothing. The ANS says the preposition in a prepositional object is set by the verb or adjective, carries little meaning of its own, and is usually the only option. That is why you cannot work it out. ANS 20.6

Learn three words as one. Not wachten. wachten op iemand. Say it with a real name.

Things become er + preposition. Ik wacht op de bus → Ik wacht erop. Waar wacht je op? The ANS describes this form: er plus the fixed preposition. People keep the pronoun: Ik wacht op hem.
