Why this lesson

Ukrainian has no articles. So every Dutch noun costs you one extra decision, and at B2 you still lose some. Onze Taal is honest about it: there are no rules, and adult learners must mostly look words up and memorise. But there are tendencies, and many sit in the last letters of the word. This lesson gives you the endings that almost settle it, so you memorise less.

Ukrainian hook. You do have gender in your head: стіл he, книга she, вікно it. It will not map. het boek is книга, she. de tafel is стіл, he. Do not transfer it. But the habit you need is the same one: look at the ending.

The idea

No rules, only tendencies. Onze Taal: natives pick it up as children; later learners look it up and memorise. The lists below "help a bit". Onze Taal, de / het

Plural is always de. het huis, de huizen. One decision less.

The article is not alone. It decides dit or deze, ons or onze, and the adjective after een: een mooi afscheid, but het mooie afscheid. One wrong article makes three wrong words.

Every row and example is from Onze Taal. The page also lists exceptions: de datum in spite of -um, de or het deksel. So: a hint is a strong bet, not a law. het meisje is a person, and still het, because the diminutive wins.
