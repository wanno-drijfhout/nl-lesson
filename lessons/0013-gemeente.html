Why this lesson

The third room in your mission: an email to the gemeente. Before you write one, you will receive one. Official letters use a small, fixed vocabulary: u dient, indien, overleggen, bezwaar maken. It looks hard and it is only a code. This lesson gives you the code for reading. Then it shows that you may write back in plain, polite Dutch. You do not have to imitate the letter.

Ukrainian hook. You know канцелярит: здійснювати, надавати, у разі потреби. Dutch officialese is the same species. Read it, decode it, do not copy it.

The idea

dienen te = moeten. Taaladvies: dienen te means moeten. It is typical of an official style and counts as formal. moeten is usable in every register and can in principle always replace it. Taaladvies

overleggen = hand in, show. In the Netherlands the standard verb for producing a diploma or other official proof is overleggen. In Belgium it is voorleggen. Taaladvies: usually stressed on the first syllable, óverleggen. Do not mix it up with overléggen, "to consult". Taaladvies

You may answer plainly. Formal vocabulary is a choice, not a duty. A clear email with u, full sentences and moeten is correct and welcome. Taaladvies, formeel / informeel

Frame for your email. Geachte heer/mevrouw, then one line saying what you want: Ik wil graag … or Hierbij …. Then the facts with dates. Then one question. Close with Met vriendelijke groet, as in lesson 0003. Four parts, done.
