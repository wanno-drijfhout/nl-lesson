What this is. One test, six mini-levels, from "I know nothing" to "I could pass as a local." Each level is a few questions. Answer what you can. Guessing is fine. Leaving blanks is fine. There is no feedback until the end, on purpose: the point is to measure, not to teach.

How to finish. At the bottom, press Show my results, then Copy results. Paste that text into the chat with your teacher (me). I will use it to write your learning records and pick lesson 0002.

Levels. The levels are the CEFR levels (Common European Framework of Reference: A1 beginner, up to C2 mastery). Every Dutch course and exam in the Netherlands uses them.

7 · Writing

Two short tasks. Write in Dutch if you can, even badly. If you cannot, write one line in English saying so. Mistakes here are the most useful data in the whole test.

Informal. Text a Dutch friend. Invite them to dinner at your place on Saturday. Three to five sentences.

Formal. Email your landlord. The heating is broken. Ask them to send someone this week. Three to five sentences.
