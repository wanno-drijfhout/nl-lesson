Why this lesson

Your mission names three rooms. This is the second one: the job interview. An interview is formal, but spoken. So it is not the letter from lesson 0003 read aloud. It has its own stock lines, its own HR words like valkuil and opzegtermijn, and one preposition question that even natives look up: solliciteren naar, op or bij? This lesson gives you nine moments and a safe line for each.

Ukrainian hook. ви maps to u, as you know. The difference is speed. A Dutch interviewer may switch to je in the first minute. Follow, do not lead.

The idea

Three prepositions, three objects. You apply naar a job, op a vacancy or an advert, bij an employer: Ik solliciteer naar de functie van planner bij de gemeente. The dictionaries Taaladvies quotes give exactly this split. solliciteren voor a job is also heard, in both countries. Taaladvies

Formal is not stiff. Taaladvies uses "formal" for forms many people feel as stiff, and "informal" for correct but loose ones. An interview sits in between: full sentences, u until invited, no slang, no derhalve. Taaladvies

u first. Start with u. When they say Zeg maar je, hoor, switch at once and stay there.
