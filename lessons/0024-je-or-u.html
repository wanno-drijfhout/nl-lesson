Why this lesson

Ukrainian has ти and ви. Dutch has je and u. Same idea. But the Dutch use u much less than you would expect.

In your placement email to the landlord you used u, and you got it right. This lesson shows the other side: when u sounds too stiff, how to switch to je, and the small verb forms that change with u.

Who gets u

In 2022 the columnist Sylvia Witteman wrote about this in Onze Taal. Her son, 21, said u to a hairdresser of about 50. She laughed at him: "U? Zeg je nou 'u' tegen me? Dan kan ik net zo goed gelijk in mijn graf gaan liggen!" ('Then I may as well lie down in my grave!')

Witteman says she hardly says u to anyone any more: not to a doctor, a dentist, a waiter or a hairdresser. Even company emails say je more and more. What once showed respect now often feels like keeping people at a distance. She keeps u for "héél oude mensen. Of de rechtbank." Very old people, or the court. (Onze Taal, Sylvia Witteman: Joe)

That is one writer's view, and a playful one. Here is a safe working rule:

Say uSay je
A formal letter or email: gemeente, landlord, job applicationFriends, family, anyone your age or younger
Someone clearly much older than you, at firstColleagues, even your boss, in most offices
A job interview, until they say jeShop staff, waiters, hairdressers, in most places
When you are not sure: start with u and let them switchAnyone who has said zeg maar je

Ukrainian hook. In Ukraine, ви is the normal start with any stranger. In the Netherlands, je is the normal start with most people under about 60. Witteman says the same about Russia, Germany and France: there, u to a stranger is normal. The Dutch are the odd ones out.

Je or jij? Use jij only with stress: Niet ik, maar jij zou het doen! Otherwise je. (Onze Taal, je/jij) The u side has no short form, so u does both jobs.

Do not mix. Pick one set for the whole message. Kunt u mij je adres sturen? must be Kunt u mij uw adres sturen?

The table layout and the do-not-mix note are the teacher's own.

Vind je, but vindt u

With je after the verb, the t drops. With u, it stays.

jeu
Vind je het leuk?Vindt u het leuk?
Ben je er morgen?Bent u er morgen?
Kun je me helpen?Kunt u me helpen?
Word je geholpen?Wordt u geholpen?

Why. Onze Taal: jij/je grew out of old ghi, which was glued onto the verb as -di (hebdi, 'have you'). That old shape left no room for a t. U started life as a third-person form (u is vriendelijk), so it takes the t in every position: bent u, vindt u. (Onze Taal, vind u/vindt u)

U hebt or u heeft? Both are right. U heeft is a bit more common. The ANS calls u heeft slightly more formal than u hebt, though Onze Taal doubts everyone feels it that way. (Onze Taal, u hebt/u heeft)

Ukrainian hook. Ви takes a plural verb: ви знаєте, never ви знаєш. Dutch u also keeps its own verb form. Think of the t as the Dutch version of that plural ending.

The examples are the teacher's own. The hook is the teacher's own.

The rule. Taaladvies: both u vergist zich and u vergist u are correct. Zich is left over from when u was third person; u fits its job as the polite je. In a command, only u works: Vergis u niet! (Taaladvies, u vergist zich/u) With hebben, all four mixes are correct, from u hebt zich vergist to the rarer u heeft u vergist. (Taaladvies, u hebt zich/u vergist)

You will see the command form on every Dutch website: Meld u aan, Log in, Houd u aan de regels.

The website examples are the teacher's own.

The switch

Usually the older person, or the one with more status, offers je. Four stock lines:

LineMeaningWho says it
Zeg maar je.Just call me je.The one who offers
Zeg maar Anna.Just call me Anna.The one who offers
Mogen we je zeggen?Shall we say je?Either; a polite question
Ik mag toch wel je zeggen?I can say je, right?Someone who expects a yes

Maar here is the friendly go-ahead from lesson 0004. Toch wel asks for a yes, like the toch tag in lesson 0022.

The verb for it: tutoyeren, to say je. The opposite is vousvoyeren, to say u. Both come from French tu and vous. (Onze Taal, vousvoyeren)

In writing. A first email to a company: u. If they reply with je, you may switch too. Many Dutch companies now write je on their own websites; that does not mean your formal letter should.

The four lines, who says them, and the writing note are the teacher's own.

First day at work

Read it aloud. Watch the moment u turns into je.

Jij: Goedemorgen. Bent u mevrouw Bakker?

Mevrouw Bakker: Ja, dat ben ik. Maar zeg maar Ingrid, hoor. En gewoon je.

Jij: Oké, Ingrid. Ik ben de nieuwe collega. Waar kan ik mijn jas laten?

Ingrid: Daar, bij de deur. Vind je het goed als ik je eerst het kantoor laat zien?

Jij: Graag. Heb jij hier ook je eerste dag gehad, of werk je hier al lang?

Ingrid: Al twintig jaar! Kom, dan stel ik je voor aan de directeur. Die zegt nog wel u, hoor.

LineWhy
Bent u mevrouw Bakker?Older, unknown, first contact: start with u. Note the t.
Zeg maar Ingrid, hoor. En gewoon je.She offers first name and je. Hoor softens it.
Vind je het goed als …'Is it OK with you if …' No t, because je follows.
Heb jij hier ook …Jij with stress: you too, like me.
Die zegt nog wel uSome people still want u. Follow their lead.
