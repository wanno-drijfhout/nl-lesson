Een besluit nemen.

Why this lesson

In the placement test you chose een besluit maken. Taaladvies is blunt about it: een besluit nemen is correct, een besluit maken is not standard Dutch, and it is probably English leaking in. That is one collocation: a noun and the verb that convention glues to it. Ukrainian has the same glue, приймати рішення, and sometimes it matches Dutch and sometimes it does not. This lesson gives you twenty pairs, sorted by verb, marked where Ukrainian helps and where English lies.

The idea

Learn the noun with its verb, never alone. besluit is half a word. een besluit nemen is the whole word. Say the pair out loud five times and it stops being a rule and becomes a sound.

Four verbs do most of the work: nemen (take), doen (do), maken (make), hebben (have). Plus a few specialists: stellen, zetten, houden, besteden, opnemen.
