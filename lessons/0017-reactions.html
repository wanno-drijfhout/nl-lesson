Why this lesson

The first goal in the mission is a relaxed, joking talk with Dutch friends. Half of that talk is not telling. It is reacting. A friend says their bike was stolen, and you have one second. A full sentence like Dat is heel vervelend voor jou is correct, but it sounds like a letter. A friend says Balen, zeg! This lesson gives you the short reactions, sorted by feeling.

Ukrainian hook. You already do this in Ukrainian: Ого!, Та ну!, Як шкода. Dutch has a word for each of these. Та ну! is almost exactly Nee joh!

The idea: one word, then wat + adjective

A Dutch reaction often has two parts. First a small sound that shows the feeling. Then wat + an adjective, with no verb.

Hè, wat jammer. · Ach, wat rot. · Goh, wat leuk.

The ANS, the big Dutch grammar, calls the small sounds expressieve tussenwerpsels and lists them by feeling (ANS 11.2.2.1). Hè, wat jammer is one of its examples. The ANS also says: many of these sounds can carry more than one feeling. The tone of your voice decides.

Friends often add zeg or joh at the end: Balen, zeg! · Wat leuk, joh! It makes the reaction warmer and more personal.

The zeg/joh tip and the four groups below are the teacher's, from daily use. The words in them are checked against the ANS list where it has them.

Four feelings

FeelingSayNote
Good newsWat leuk! · Wat goed! · Gaaf! · Vet!Vet is young slang for 'great'; Onze Taal noted it in 1995. Safest with friends your own age.
Bad newsBalen! · Wat jammer. · Wat rot voor je. · Ach.Balen = be fed up, gutted. Ach and och show pity or sorrow (ANS).
SurpriseEcht? · Goh! · Jeetje! · Tjonge! · Nee joh!Goh and tjonge are in the ANS list for surprise. Jeetje is a softened Jezus (ANS).
ReliefOef! · Hèhè!Hèhè = 'finally!' After a long wait.

Two to handle with care. Boeien! means 'who cares'. Lekker belangrijk means the same, with irony: 'oh, how very important'. Onze Taal traced both in 2006. Use them about a thing, never about your friend's news, or you are rude.

Where balen comes from: ergens balen tabak van hebben, 'to have bales of tobacco of it', a joke about having too much of a good thing (Onze Taal). Stronger still: balen als een stekker. Als een stekker adds force but means nothing.

Register. All of this is for je and jij. To someone you call u, use the calm ones: Wat vervelend voor u. · Wat jammer. · Helaas. Never balen or boeien to the gemeente.

Look at Nee joh: it does two jobs. After news it means 'no way!'. After a sorry it means 'don't worry'. A Dutch reaction often ends with a question, like Waar dan? That keeps the talk going.
