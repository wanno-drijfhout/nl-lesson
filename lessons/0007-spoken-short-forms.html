Why this lesson

You read Dutch at B2. Then a native speaks at normal speed and half the words vanish. They did not vanish. They shrank. Small words without stress get a short form: ik becomes 'k, hem becomes 'm, mijn becomes m'n. Once you know the short forms, your ear finds the word borders again. This is the listening lesson. It also fixes a writing trap: me zusje.

Ukrainian hook. Ukrainian can simply drop the pronoun: Бачив його. Dutch cannot drop it. So Dutch squeezes it instead. Where your ear expects nothing, listen for one tiny sound.

The idea

Stress decides. Most pronouns have a full form and a reduced form. With stress, only the full form works: Niet hij maar zij heeft gebeld. Without stress, speech uses the short one. Taaladvies

Two groups in writing. je, we, ze, me are normal in any text. The apostrophe forms, 'k, 't, 'm, m'n, z'n, d'r, are informal speech written down: fine in a text message, wrong in a letter. Onze Taal

The trap. me zusje is not correct. me is the short form of mij, not of mijn. Write mijn zusje or m'n zusje. Onze Taal

How to train your ear. Pick one short form per day. Play any Dutch video. Tap the table each time you hear it. After a week the "missing" words are back.
