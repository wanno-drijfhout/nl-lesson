Why this lesson

Your formal email from lesson 0003 has the right frame. Inside the frame, your sentences still stand alone: fact, fact, request. Native writing ties them together with small linking words: echter, bovendien, daardoor, namelijk. They are the difference between a list and an argument. This lesson gives you the set, the word order after each, and the two that natives themselves get wrong.

Ukrainian hook. You already write this way in Ukrainian: однак, крім того, адже, отже. The meanings carry over one to one. The only new thing is Dutch word order: the verb must stay second.

The idea

A connector in first place takes the verb with it. Bovendien is het te duur. Daarom kom ik later. The connector fills the first slot, the verb comes second, the subject third. You know this rule. You only have to keep it when the first word is fancy.

echter is the exception. Safest in the middle: Wij kunnen echter niet garanderen dat … At the start it takes a comma and normal order: Echter, wij kunnen niet … The version with inversion, Echter kunnen wij …, is not accepted by everyone. Taaladvies

daarom is a reason, daardoor is a cause. A reason involves a human choice; a cause does not. Strict writers keep daardoor for causes only. daarom is used for both in practice. Taaladvies
