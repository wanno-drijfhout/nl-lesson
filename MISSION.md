# Mission: Dutch (Nederlands)

## Why
The learner may live in the Netherlands one day and wants Dutch in the toolbox before that happens. The goal is not survival Dutch: it is passing as a native in a job interview, at a birthday party, and in an email to the gemeente.

## Success looks like
- Hold a relaxed, joking conversation with Dutch friends using natural informal Dutch (je/jij, slang, particles like *even*, *toch*, *hoor*).
- Write a formal letter or email (to an employer, landlord, or government body) with correct register (*u*, *Geachte*, formal vocabulary) and no spelling errors.
- Read a Dutch newspaper (NRC, Volkskrant) and a novel without a dictionary.
- Follow fast native speech on TV, radio, and podcasts.
- Pass Staatsexamen NT2 Programma II (B2) as a milestone on the way to C1/C2.
